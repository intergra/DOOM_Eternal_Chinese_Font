This mod uses the Bold and Medium OTF fonts from Source Han Sans SC 2.005R to
rebuild Simplified Chinese and safely replaceable ordinary-text glyphs in the
game's two target font atlases. The original font-data records, metrics,
character ordering, duplicate mappings, protected game/navigation symbols,
Source Han-uncovered glyphs and empty slots are preserved.
